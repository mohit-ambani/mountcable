Three things decide the number. The RCCB is the single largest item, so choose the rating you need (40 A covers most homes) and the AC type unless you run a solar inverter, where the HPI type is worth its premium. Every outgoing MCB from 6 to 32 A costs the same ₹494, so there is no saving in under-sizing a circuit and no penalty in sizing it correctly. The board price steps up with the door: IP30 for a cupboard, IP43 metal door for the usual indoor position, acrylic for looks, IP54 for damp locations. The MCB selector gives the rating for each circuit and the DB selection guide the number of ways.

How to get the lowest Legrand switchgear price

  1. Send the DB schedule, or a photo of the electrician's list: incomer rating, RCCB rating and sensitivity, each outgoing MCB by rating and pole, and the DB size and door.
  2. Add the switches so the house is one Legrand order. Stockists buy Legrand by the carton and price a full order better than a DB alone.
  3. Ask for stock items. C-curve DX3 in standard ratings, AC-type RCCBs and Ekinox SPN boards are stocked and priced best; special ratings and D-curve above 32 A are ordered from Legrand.
  4. Compare on MRP. Every quote you get should be readable against the tables above. A quote far below ours is not a better deal on the same product; read Legrand MCB 100% original.
