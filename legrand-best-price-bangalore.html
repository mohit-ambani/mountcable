Why a stockist's Legrand price is lower

Legrand's Indian channel is manufacturer, authorised distributor, dealer, buyer. A stockist buys from the distributor in carton quantities across the range and turns the stock quickly, so the buying price is lower and the margin needed per piece is smaller. A shop that keeps a few Legrand boxes buys them from the same distributor, or from a stockist, in small lots, and each piece carries more cost. Both sell below MRP; the stockist sells further below. That is the whole of the argument, and it is why Mount Cable India can give 65% off MRP on every Legrand product, one of the highest Legrand discounts a Bangalore buyer will find for original stock.

What a too-low Legrand price means

There is a floor under every genuine Legrand price: the distributor's price to the dealer. Our 65% off MRP sits close to that floor, and nobody sells original DX3 or Myrius much below it for long. A quote well under a stockist's net price is therefore one of three things: a copy, which is common for DX3 MCBs and Myrius plates; returned or damaged stock; or grey stock that came from outside the Indian channel and carries no replacement policy.
