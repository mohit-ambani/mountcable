When Finoultra is worth it

Choose Finoultra where a halogen-free specification is written (premium apartments, villas, offices), for long AC and geyser runs where heat resistance matters, and anywhere you want the best Finolex makes. For a standard home, Flamegard FR-LSH gives most of the smoke-safety benefit at a lower price. The full comparison is in FR vs FR-LSH vs Finoultra.
