Is a very cheap Schneider MCB genuine?
Almost never. With 3–5% margins in switchgear, genuine dealers cannot fund deep discounts. Counterfeit MCBs are dangerous because they may not trip during a fault.
Which Schneider RCCB rating should a home use?
Use a 30mA DP RCCB for a single-phase home — 40A or 63A depending on load. 30mA sensitivity is what protects people from electric shock.
What is the difference between B-curve and C-curve MCBs?
C-curve MCBs tolerate brief inrush currents from motors, ACs and pumps and are standard in homes; B-curve trips faster and suits pure lighting or electronic loads.
