How to price a Legrand switchboard

Add the mechanisms, then the plate, then the box. A typical bedroom board of two 6A switches, one fan regulator and one 6/16A socket in Lyncus is two at ₹100, one at ₹734 and one at ₹434 for the mechanisms, a 6-module classic white plate at ₹332 and a 6-module box at ₹226: ₹1,926 MRP for the board, before the dealer discount. The same board in Britzy is about ₹1,600 and in Myrius about ₹2,900. Multiply by the number of boards from the material list builder and you have the switch budget for the house.

Which Legrand range to choose

Britzy and Allzy give you the Legrand mechanism at the lowest cost and are the right choice for rental flats, utility areas and budget builds. Lyncus adds a wider colour palette of plates, BLDC fan regulators and USB-C chargers at a moderate premium. Myrius is the range specified in premium apartments, with glass and metallic plates and the widest set of hospitality and automation modules. The modular switch brands guide compares Legrand with Anchor, Schneider and Greatwhite.
