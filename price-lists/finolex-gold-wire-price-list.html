What you get for the difference

Both packs are IS 694 flame-retardant single-core copper wire from Finolex, and both carry the same QR verification. Gold is positioned as the premium pack with the higher insulation specification and the premium carton, and it is what most electricians in Bangalore ask for by name on a new house. Silver is the standard pack and is the right choice where the budget is tight or the run is short. The full argument, including what an electrician means when he says one is better, is in Finolex Silver vs Gold 90 m.

Gold Pack by size: what each is used for

  • 1.0 sq mm: earth wire on lighting circuits and low-load points.
  • 1.5 sq mm: lighting circuits and 6A socket points.
  • 2.5 sq mm: 16A socket circuits, kitchen points, mixer and microwave.
  • 4.0 sq mm: geyser and 1.5-ton AC points.
  • 6.0 sq mm: 2-ton AC points and the main feed from meter to DB.

Gold Pack coils are 90 m only. If your run needs longer coils, the same wire in the FR 180 m project pack and 300 m / 200 m long coils is on the full Finolex price list.

FAQs

What is the Finolex Gold wire price for 1.5 sq mm?
A Finolex Gold Pack 1.5 sq mm 90 m coil is ₹2,536 at Mount Cable India, against ₹2,257 for the Silver Pack, current to 4 September 2026, taxes extra.
Is Finolex Gold better than Finolex Silver?
Gold is Finolex's premium FR pack and Silver is the standard FR pack. Both are genuine IS 694 copper wire with the same QR verification. Gold carries the higher specification and price; Silver is the value choice for the same sizes.
Does Finolex Gold come in 180 m or 300 m coils?
No. The Gold Pack is a 90 m coil. The 180 m project pack and the 300 m / 200 m long coils are supplied as FR and FR-LSH wire, listed on the full Finolex price list.
Is a Finolex Gold coil sold far below this price genuine?
Treat it with suspicion. Wire is a low-margin trade and Gold Pack cartons are the most copied. Scan both QR codes on the carton before paying, or bring the coil to us and we will check it at no charge.
