Reading the Polycab price list

Polycab publishes several lists rather than one: a coil list for house wire, a per-metre list for flexible and FRLS cable, a per-coil list for co-axial and speaker cable, and a per-metre list for armoured power cable. That is why the units change between the tables above. When you compare Polycab with Finolex, compare coil to coil: a 90 m Etira coil against a 90 m Finolex Silver Pack coil of the same size. The four-brand wire comparison and RR Kabel vs Polycab vs Finolex do this size by size.

Which Polycab range for a house

Etira FR is the standard house wire and the one most electricians mean when they say Polycab. Primma and Optima add the low-flame FRLF property for flats and enclosed spaces, and Maxima HR-FR adds heat resistance for kitchens, geyser points and long AC runs. For apartment projects the 300 m and 200 m coils bring the per-metre cost down by roughly 10 percent against 90 m coils.

Polycab duplicates

Polycab is the largest wire brand in India and the second most copied after Finolex. Every genuine Polycab coil carries the brand, size, ISI mark and a batch code printed along the wire, and the carton carries a hologram and a verification code. A list-price coil offered at half the figures above is the clearest warning. Our guide to identifying duplicate Polycab wire walks through the checks.

What is the difference between Polycab Etira, Primma, Optima and Maxima?
Etira is standard FR house wire. Primma and Optima are FRLF (flame retardant low flame) grades with better fire performance. Maxima is HR-FR, heat resistant for kitchens, geysers and long AC runs. All are IS 694 copper wire in 90 m coils.
