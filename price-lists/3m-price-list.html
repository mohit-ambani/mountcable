Usually not. 3M is one of the most-faked tape brands in India, and genuine sellers on 3–5% margins cannot halve prices. Fake tape loses adhesion and insulation quickly.
What is the difference between Super 33+ and Temflex tape?
Super 33+ is the professional-grade all-weather vinyl tape rated for -18 to 105 degrees C; Temflex is the economy general-purpose tape for basic indoor insulation.
Which 3M product should I use for outdoor cable joints?
Wrap with Scotch 23 rubber splicing tape for insulation build-up and finish with Super 33+ on top, or use a 3M LV splice kit for buried or exposed joints.
