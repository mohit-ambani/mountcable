Flexible cable or house wire?

Concealed wiring in conduit uses single-core house wire. Flexible cable is for anything that moves or is handled: appliance leads, extension boards, panel wiring, motors. Three-core flexible is the usual choice for a washing machine or water purifier point fed from a socket. For pumps see the Finolex submersible cable price list.
