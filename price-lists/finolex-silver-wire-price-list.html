Silver Pack or a longer coil?

For a single house the 90 m Silver Pack is the convenient size: an electrician can carry it, and a 2BHK uses a handful of coils per size. For apartments and row houses the 180 m project pack and the 300 m / 200 m long coils cut the price per metre and the number of joints. The 45 m coil is for repairs, extensions and one-room jobs.

Silver or Gold?

Both are genuine IS 694 FR copper house wire with the same two-QR verification on the carton. Gold is Finolex's premium pack and costs a little more per coil; the per-size gap is on the Finolex Gold price list and explained in Silver vs Gold.

Is Finolex Silver good for house wiring?
Yes. Silver Pack is IS 694 flame-retardant copper house wire, and it is what most homes in Bangalore are wired with. Choose Flamegard FR-LSH instead if you want low smoke in a fire, which we recommend for flats and bedrooms.
Is a Finolex Silver coil far below this price genuine?
Be careful. Wire is a 3 to 5 percent margin trade, so a coil far below a distributor's price is usually short in length, thin in copper or counterfeit. Scan both QR codes on the carton before you pay.
