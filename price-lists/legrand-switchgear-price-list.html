RCCB or RCBO

An RCCB protects a whole board against earth leakage and shock; it does not protect against overload, which the MCBs downstream do. An RCBO combines both in one device for one circuit, and is specified where a single leaky circuit should trip alone rather than darken the house. For most Bangalore homes one 30 mA DP RCCB as incomer plus MCBs per circuit is the standard, with an RCBO on the geyser or the outdoor circuit if the budget allows. The MCB, RCCB and RCBO guide has the reasoning.

Choosing the Ekinox board

Count the outgoing circuits, add two spare ways, and that is the SPN size. IP43 with a metal door is the normal indoor specification; acrylic doors are chosen for looks in a living area; IP54 is for damp or dusty locations such as a basement or a terrace pump room. A three-phase connection needs an ETPN or vertical TPN board instead. See the distribution board selection guide.

What is the Legrand 40A RCCB price?
A DX3 double-pole 40A 30 mA AC-type RCCB is ₹6,360 MRP; the four-pole 40A 30 mA is ₹8,068. HPI 40A units are ₹12,271 (DP) and ₹13,657 (FP).

A DX3 double-pole 30 mA RCBO is ₹6,974 MRP from 6A to 32A, ₹8,380 at 40A and ₹10,210 at 63A. The compact 2-module DP RCBO 6 to 32A is ₹8,291.

What is the price of a Legrand TPN distribution board?

What is the difference between AC type, Type S and HPI RCCBs?
AC type senses standard alternating leakage and is the residential default. Type S has a delayed trip so it coordinates with downstream RCDs. HPI has high immunity to nuisance tripping and detects DC-component faults from inverters and electronics.
