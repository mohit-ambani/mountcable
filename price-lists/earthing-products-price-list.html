Is a very cheap copper earthing rod genuine?
Usually not. With 3–5% margins nobody can halve the price of genuine copper-bonded rods; cheap ones have micron-thin coating over steel and fail early.
How many earthing pits does a house in Bangalore need?
A typical independent house needs at least two pits — one for the mains and one for appliance/body earthing — and a third if you install a lightning arrester or heavy borewell pump.
What is the difference between GI and copper-bonded earthing?
GI electrodes are cheaper but corrode faster, lasting 5–10 years; copper-bonded electrodes with chemical backfill last 20+ years and hold lower resistance in dry Bangalore soil.
