Is very cheap Finolex wire genuine?
Usually not. Wires run on 3–5% dealer margins, so no genuine seller can go far below market rate. A too-good price is the single biggest signal of duplicate Finolex stock.
What is the difference between Finolex FR and FRLS wire?
FR (Flame Retardant) resists catching fire; FRLS (Flame Retardant Low Smoke) additionally emits far less smoke during a fire, which matters in closed apartments. FRLS costs about 8–12% more per coil.
Which Finolex wire size should I use for AC and geyser points?
Use 4.0 sq mm for most 1.5-ton ACs and geysers, and 6.0 sq mm for 2-ton ACs or long runs. Lighting circuits use 1.5 sq mm and plug points 2.5 sq mm.
