How to use this Finolex price list

Start from the size, not the range. Lighting circuits use 1.5 sq mm, 6A socket circuits 2.5 sq mm, geyser and 1.5-ton AC points 4.0 sq mm, and the main feed or a 2-ton AC 6.0 sq mm. Then pick the pack: Silver Pack for standard FR house wire, Gold Pack for the premium line, Flamegard FR-LSH for flats and enclosed stairwells where smoke matters, and Finoultra where a halogen-free specification is written. Work out how many metres you need with the 2BHK/3BHK wire quantity guide or the house wiring cost calculator.

Why our Finolex price is lower than the shop near you

Finolex sells through distributors, and a distributor buys in truckloads directly from the company. A retailer buys a few cartons from a distributor and adds a margin on top. Mount Cable India is the distributor, so the price you see here is one step closer to the factory than a shop price. The catch in the wire trade is that a price far below a distributor's is not a bargain: it is short length, thin copper or a counterfeit carton.
