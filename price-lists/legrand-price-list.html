How Legrand pricing works

Legrand publishes one MPL for India each year and revises it mid-year when needed. Every item has an MRP printed on the pack, and every dealer sells below that MRP; how far below depends on the range, the quantity and the dealer's own buying. That is why two shops can quote different figures for the same DX3 MCB and both be genuine. What no genuine dealer can do is sell a Legrand RCCB at a third of MRP: at that level the device is either a copy or a returned unit. How to identify a duplicate Legrand MCB covers the checks.
