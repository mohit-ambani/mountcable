How Legrand pricing works

Legrand publishes one MPL for India each year and revises it mid-year when needed. Every item has an MRP printed on the pack, and every dealer sells below that MRP; how far below depends on the range, the quantity and the dealer's own buying. That is why two shops can quote different figures for the same DX3 MCB and both be genuine. Mount Cable India's rate is 65% off MRP across the range, which is close to the floor a stockist can offer on original stock; a quote well below that level is a copy, a returned unit or grey stock without warranty.
