Which Anchor range to choose

Roma Classic is the value modular range: a 10AX switch at ₹77 MRP, familiar to every electrician in Bangalore, with the widest spares availability. Roma Urban is the current design range with the curved plate and 1-module and 2-module switch options at about double the price. Penta Modular sits below Roma Classic in price and above the piano Penta range, which is for wooden boards and budget builds. For a new house we usually quote Roma Urban in the living areas and Roma Classic or Penta Modular elsewhere. The switch brand guide and Greatwhite vs Anchor compare it with the other brands we stock.

Anchor plates and the module system

Anchor prices the switch module and the cover plate separately. A 6-module Roma Urban board with three 10AX switches, a fan regulator and a 6/16A socket is three at ₹160, one at ₹785 and one at ₹504 plus a ₹470 plate: ₹2,239 MRP. The same in Roma Classic with a Tresa or Deko plate comes to about ₹1,500. Count your boards with the material list builder.
