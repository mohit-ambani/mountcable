Anchor UNO against Legrand and Schneider

On MRP, a UNO 10 kA single-pole MCB at ₹308 is about 60 percent of a Legrand DX3 at ₹494, and the UNO 40A DP 30 mA RCCB at ₹3,568 is well under the DX3's ₹6,360. Both carry the ISI mark and the same 10 kA breaking capacity. The difference is in the build, the terminal design and the accessories range. Note that Mount Cable India sells Legrand at 65% off MRP, which brings a DX3 single pole to ₹173 net, so compare net prices rather than MRPs before deciding. For a rental flat or a budget home the UNO is a sound choice; for a premium build most buyers stay with the European brands. Sizing the board

Use the MCB selector for each circuit and the load calculator for the incomer, then add two spare ways. An 8-way SPN metal DB at ₹2,475 MRP covers a typical 2BHK; a 12-way at ₹3,069 a 3BHK.

What is the Anchor DB price?

Is Anchor UNO switchgear as safe as Legrand?
Both are ISI-marked 10 kA MCBs to IS/IEC 60898-1 and both trip on the same overload and short-circuit curves. Legrand offers more refined terminals, accessories and a wider range. For residential use either protects the circuit correctly when correctly sized.
