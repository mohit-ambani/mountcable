Is very cheap conduit pipe worth buying?
No. At 3–5% margins genuine ISI conduit cannot be dumped cheap; ultra-low prices mean thin, brittle non-ISI pipe that cracks in slabs and cannot be rewired later.
What is the difference between MMS and HMS conduit?
MMS is medium mechanical strength for walls and ceilings; HMS is heavy gauge for floor slabs and areas that take compaction load. Slab work should always use HMS.
