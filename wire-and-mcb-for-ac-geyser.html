Which wire and MCB for an AC and geyser

ACs and geysers are the heaviest loads in a house and the circuits most often under-wired. Here is the pairing we supply.

Why heat matters here

These circuits carry high current for hours. That is where an under-sized or counterfeit wire runs hot, so it is where the wire grade, the size and a genuine coil matter most. For the longest runs, Finoultra's heat-resistant e-beam insulation is worth considering.

Questions, Answered

Frequently asked questions

Which wire is used for AC?
4.0 sq mm Finolex FR-LSH for a 1.5-ton AC and 6.0 sq mm for a 2-ton AC, each on its own Legrand DP MCB.
Which wire for a geyser?
4.0 sq mm Finolex FR-LSH on a Legrand DX3 DP 20 A MCB.
Is 2.5 sq mm wire enough for an AC?
We do not recommend it for a 1.5-ton or larger AC. Use 4.0 sq mm, or 6.0 sq mm for 2-ton.
