Questions, Answered

Frequently asked questions

Which wire is best for house wiring?
Finolex Flamegard FR-LSH, correctly sized: 1.5 sq mm for lights, 2.5 for sockets, 4.0 for geysers and 1.5-ton ACs, 6.0 for 2-ton ACs and the main feed.
Which is better, FR or FRLS wire?
FRLS (FR-LSH) gives off much less smoke in a fire, at a small extra cost per coil. We recommend it for most homes.
Is copper wire better than aluminium for houses?
Yes. Copper is the standard for house wiring; aluminium is used for large service cables, not internal circuits.
