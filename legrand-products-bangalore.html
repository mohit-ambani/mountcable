Stock items and order items

Legrand marks the items it keeps with its stockists in bold in the MPL, and states that the rest are delivered within 4 to 8 weeks from the date of order. In practice that means:

  • On our shelf: DX3 C-curve MCBs in every pole from 6 to 63 A, DX3 AC-type RCCBs at 25, 40 and 63 A in 30 and 100 mA, DP and FP isolators, Ekinox SPN boards in every size and door, ETPN and vertical TPN boards in the common sizes, and the Britzy, Allzy, Lyncus and Myrius mechanisms and plates in every module count and the main finishes.
  • Ordered from Legrand: D-curve MCBs above 32 A, DC MCBs, Type S and HPI RCCBs in some ratings, 80 to 125 A breakers, SPDs, contactors and time switches, DPX3 MCCBs, Arteor and Arteor Advance, Mylinc, Myrius glass and IoT, cable management, and any plate finish outside the main ones.

If your specification names an order item, send it early; we place the order with Legrand and hold the stock items until the whole list is ready to deliver together.

How to order by cat number

Every Legrand product has a six-digit cat number printed as two groups of digits, and it is the only reliable way to specify an item across the range: 4085 90 is a 10 A single-pole C-curve DX3, 4118 52 a 40 A DP 30 mA RCCB, 5076 12 a 12-way IP43 SPN Ekinox board, 6772 00 a Lyncus 6A one-way switch, 6775 06 a Lyncus 6-module classic white plate. Consultants' BOMs and builders' specifications carry these numbers. Send the list as it is and we quote line by line against the MPL, with the MRP shown for each cat number and our price below it. If a list is written in words instead, we translate it to cat numbers on the quote so there is no substitution.
