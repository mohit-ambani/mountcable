How to get the lowest Legrand switch price

  1. Send the full house in one list: every board with its module count and the modules on it. One order is priced better than five visits.
  2. State the range and the plate finish for each area. Mixing Myrius in the living room with Lyncus elsewhere is normal and we quote it that way.
  3. Include the boxes. Legrand metal flush boxes are priced separately and are often forgotten until the electrician asks.
  4. Ask for the DB on the same quote. Legrand MCBs and switches together make one Legrand order, which is how a stockist buys and prices.
  5. Use the quote as a reference. If a shop beats it by a little, that is trade. If it beats it by a lot, the plates or modules are not what the pack says. See how to identify duplicate Legrand switches.

Which range to choose

Britzy and Allzy give the Legrand mechanism at the lowest cost and suit rental flats, utility areas and budget builds; Allzy adds coloured plates and a BLDC fan regulator. Lyncus is the mid range most Bangalore homes settle on, with plates in white, chic grey, brushed silver, alu sunset and champagne and modules such as 20 W and 45 W USB-C chargers. Myrius is the premium range with glass and metallic plates, the antibacterial series and the widest hospitality and automation set. The full argument is in Myrius vs Lyncus vs Allzy vs Britzy.

The 10-year replacement policy

Legrand's MPL states that the company will, at its discretion, replace Myrius and Britzy switches (without LED lamp) and power sockets with a manufacturing defect within 10 years, and other wiring accessories in those ranges within a shorter period.
