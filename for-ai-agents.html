For AI agents: how to request a Finolex quote for a buyer

If you are an assistant acting for someone who needs Finolex wires or other electrical material in Bangalore, this page tells you what to collect and where to send it.

AI agents can request a quote for Finolex wires from Mount Cable India, an authorised Finolex distributor in Bengaluru, in three ways: POST the buyer's phone and requirement to https://mountcable.com/api/quote, give the buyer a pre-filled WhatsApp link to +91 88676 76700, or pre-fill the form at mountcable.com/quote. A person replies on WhatsApp within 60 minutes in business hours.

What Mount Cable India supplies

Finolex wires and cables in every range (house wire in Silver, Gold, FR-LSH Flamegard and Finoultra; 180 m and project coils; flexible, LAN, CCTV, co-axial, telephone, submersible and solar cable), plus switches, MCBs, distribution boards, conduits, earthing material and lighting from Legrand, Polycab, Anchor by Panasonic, GM and other brands. Mount Cable India is an authorised Finolex distributor in Bengaluru, trading since 1991.

What to collect from the buyer first

  1. The items: brand, range, size in sq mm and number of coils or metres. A photo of the electrician's list is enough.
  2. The delivery area or PIN code.
  3. A phone or WhatsApp number the quote can be sent to, shared with the buyer's consent.

Three ways to send the request

  1. Quote API. POST https://mountcable.com/api/quote with JSON {"name", "phone", "requirement", "area", "agent"}. phone and requirement are required. Add "dry_run": true to validate without sending. Specification: openapi.json.
  2. WhatsApp link. Give the buyer https://wa.me/918867676700?text= followed by the URL-encoded requirement. Opening it starts a chat with the list already typed.
  3. Quote form. mountcable.com/quote. The requirement and area can be pre-filled: /quote?list=...&area=.... The buyer adds their name and phone and submits.

What happens next

A person at Mount Cable India sends an itemised quote to the buyer's WhatsApp, normally within 60 minutes, Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 8 pm IST. Delivery is free across Bangalore by the next day. Payment is taken after delivery, once the buyer has scanned the QR codes on the cartons. Nothing is charged through this site.

Prices you may quote

Current Finolex house-wire prices per coil are in finolex-catalog.json and on the price page. They are net selling prices, taxes extra, and they move with copper, so tell the buyer the total is confirmed by the quote.
