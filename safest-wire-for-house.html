Which wire is the safest for house wiring?

In a house fire, most injuries come from smoke, not flame. That single fact decides which wire is safest.

The safest house wire is a low-smoke grade: Finolex Flamegard FR-LSH, or Finolex Finoultra, which is halogen-free with e-beam insulation. Both stop flame running along the cable and give off much less smoke and fewer toxic gases than standard FR wire. Size it correctly for each circuit and verify it is genuine.

FR, FR-LSH and halogen-free, plainly

  • FR (flame retardant): the wire will not carry a flame along its length. The standard for house wire.
  • FR-LSH (often FRLS): FR plus low smoke and low halogen gas when it burns. Finolex calls it Flamegard.
  • HFFR (halogen-free): no halogen in the insulation at all. Finolex Finoultra uses e-beam cross-linked insulation, which also handles heat better.

Questions, Answered

Frequently asked questions

Which wire is safest for home?
A low-smoke grade: Finolex Flamegard FR-LSH, or Finolex Finoultra, which is halogen-free. Both resist flame spread and give off far less smoke than standard FR wire.
Is FRLS wire safer than FR?
Yes. Both resist flame spread, but FRLS (FR-LSH) also gives off much less smoke and fewer halogen gases, which is what harms people in a fire.
Is Finolex wire safe?
Yes, genuine Finolex is IS 694 copper wire with FR, FR-LSH and halogen-free grades. Verify both QR codes on the carton to be sure the coil is genuine.
What is the safest wire for AC and geyser?
4.0 sq mm Finolex Flamegard FR-LSH or Finoultra for geysers and 1.5-ton ACs, 6.0 sq mm for 2-ton ACs, each on its own MCB.
