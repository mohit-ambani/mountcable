Safety · Finolex wire + Legrand protection

Which is the safest electrical brand for your home?

Electrical safety is not one product. It is the wire that does not spread fire or smoke, the breaker that trips before the wire overheats, the RCCB that trips before a shock hurts someone, and the socket that keeps little fingers out. Here is how we build a safe house, and the brands we use.

For safety we recommend Finolex Flamegard FR-LSH or Finoultra wire, which limit flame spread and give off far less smoke in a fire, with Legrand protection: DX3 MCBs sized to the wire, a 30 mA Legrand RCCB on the incomer, and Legrand shuttered sockets. Above all, buy genuine: a counterfeit of any brand is the least safe product in the house.

The four layers of a safe installation

  1. Wire that resists fire and smoke: Finolex Flamegard FR-LSH or Finoultra.
  2. Breakers that protect the wire: Legrand DX3 MCBs, each sized to the wire on its circuit.
  3. Protection for people: a 30 mA Legrand RCCB, which trips on earth leakage in milliseconds.
  4. Safe points: Legrand shuttered sockets and a proper earth pit.

Genuine is the biggest safety factor

A duplicate wire has less copper than the label says, so it runs hot on a load it should carry. A duplicate MCB may not trip at all. That is why we tell buyers to verify every Finolex coil with both QR codes and to buy Legrand from a stockist at a sensible price.

Finolex cartons carry two QR codes: an outer code on the carton label and an inner code you reach only after opening. The outer one proves the carton is registered with Finolex; the inner one proves the contents are what Finolex packed. Scan both, at your site, before you pay. Questions, Answered

Frequently asked questions

Which electrical brand is safest?
No brand is safe if it is counterfeit or wrongly sized. We recommend Finolex FR-LSH wire and Legrand MCBs, RCCB and shuttered sockets, bought genuine and installed to the right sizes.
Is an RCCB necessary in a house?
Yes. A 30 mA RCCB is the device that protects people from electric shock. MCBs protect the wire, not the person.
Which wire is safest for a house?
Finolex Flamegard FR-LSH, or Finoultra for halogen-free. Both resist flame spread and give off much less smoke than standard FR wire.
