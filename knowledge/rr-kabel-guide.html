RR Kabel: The Complete Brand Guide

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

RR Kabel is one of the fastest-growing names in Indian house wiring and has positioned itself squarely on safety and conductor technology. Among Bangalore electricians it has moved from "alternative brand" to first-choice specification in many projects, especially apartments.

Company snapshot

RR Kabel is part of the Mumbai-based Ram Ratna group and is a listed Indian company. It manufactures wires and cables for homes, industry and export, and has been particularly vocal about wire safety standards in its category. Its export footprint is significant, which in practice means its plants run to certifications demanded by overseas buyers.

RR Kabel's electrical product ranges

  • Superex FR — RR Kabel's mainstream flame-retardant house wire, the direct competitor to Finolex FR and Polycab FR.
  • Firex LS0H — the premium safety line: low smoke, zero halogen insulation that emits minimal toxic gas in a fire. The strongest choice for flats, high-rises and basements.
  • Unilay conductor design — RR Kabel's compacted multi-strand conductor layout, which gives a rounder, more uniform conductor for the same copper.
  • Flexible multicore cables — for pumps, appliances and panels; RR Kabel also markets a wider FMEG range including fans and switches.

What RR Kabel is best at

RR Kabel's sweet spot is the safety-conscious home buyer. If your project is an apartment or any enclosed space, Firex LS0H is one of the best insulation specifications you can buy in the Indian retail market. Superex keeps the brand competitive in the mainstream band. Pricing sits alongside Finolex and Polycab — this is a premium brand, chosen on specification rather than discount. How to make sure your RR Kabel product is genuine

  • Check the wire print: brand, series name, size and ISI mark should repeat cleanly along the length.
  • Match the series on the carton to what you are paying for — Superex and Firex are different products at different prices.
  • Scan the verification QR where provided on the pack and confirm the details.

Questions, Answered

RR Kabel FAQs

What is the difference between RR Kabel Superex and Firex?
Superex is RR Kabel's mainstream flame-retardant (FR) house wire, while Firex is the premium LS0H line with low-smoke, zero-halogen insulation that releases minimal toxic gas in a fire. Firex is the better choice for apartments, high-rises and basements.
Is RR Kabel as good as Finolex or Polycab?
Yes. RR Kabel competes in the same premium band as Finolex and Polycab, and its Firex LS0H line is among the strongest safety specifications available in Indian retail house wiring.
What is unilay wire in RR Kabel?
Unilay is RR Kabel's compacted conductor design in which the copper strands are laid in a uniform pattern, giving a rounder, more consistent conductor. It improves uniformity for the same copper content.
