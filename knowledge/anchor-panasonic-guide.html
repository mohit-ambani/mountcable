Anchor by Panasonic: The Complete Brand Guide

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Anchor is arguably the most widely installed switch brand in India. Walk into almost any home built in the last two decades and there is a fair chance the switchboards carry Anchor plates. Since Panasonic acquired the company, the brand trades as Anchor by Panasonic, combining Indian market reach with Japanese ownership.

Company snapshot

Anchor Electricals was built in Mumbai into India's highest-volume switch maker before being acquired by Panasonic. Today Anchor by Panasonic manufactures switches, wiring accessories, wires, lighting and fans at large Indian plants. Its distribution reach is enormous — which is precisely why it is also heavily counterfeited.

Anchor by Panasonic's electrical product ranges

  • Roma — the flagship modular switch range and one of India's best-selling switch series; wide accessory choice, easy availability of spares for decades.
  • Penta — the value line, popular for rental properties and budget projects; available in modular and traditional formats.
  • Ziva — a newer, contemporary modular range with a sleeker look at a mid price.
  • Wires and accessories — Anchor also sells house wires, MCBs, doorbells, holders and lighting, letting small jobs run on one brand.

What Anchor by Panasonic is best at

Anchor's superpower is ubiquity. Roma modules are so widely stocked that a broken switch can be replaced from any electrical shop in Bangalore, today and ten years from now. That makes Roma the pragmatic choice for most homes: good looks, proven reliability, effortless spares. Penta suits tight budgets; Ziva suits buyers who want a fresher aesthetic without moving to premium-priced European ranges like Schneider or Legrand.

How to make sure your Anchor by Panasonic product is genuine

  • High-volume switch brands are the most copied products in the electrical trade. Check moulding quality: genuine plates have clean edges, uniform colour and crisp brand marking on the back.
  • Check the packaging print and that the range name (Roma, Penta, Ziva) matches what you are paying for.
  • Scan the verification QR where provided on the pack.
  • Buy from an authorised channel at market-band prices. With 3–5% trade margins, a big discount on Anchor stock signals a duplicate — our original vs duplicate guide explains what fails first in fake switches.

Anchor by Panasonic FAQs

Which Anchor switch range is best for a home?
Anchor Roma is the best all-round choice for most homes: proven reliability, a huge accessory range, and spares available everywhere in India for years. Choose Penta for tight budgets and Ziva for a more contemporary look at a mid price.
Is Anchor owned by Panasonic?
Yes. Anchor Electricals was acquired by Panasonic and now trades as Anchor by Panasonic, combining Anchor's Indian manufacturing and distribution with Panasonic ownership.
How do I identify a fake Anchor switch?
Genuine Anchor switches have clean moulding, uniform colour, crisp brand marking on the back and quality packaging print. Buy from an authorised dealer at market-band prices — steeply discounted switch lots are the most commonly counterfeited items in the electrical trade.
