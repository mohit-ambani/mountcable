Greatwhite: The Complete Brand Guide

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Greatwhite is one of the fastest-rising switch and electrical-accessory brands in India. It has carved out a clear position: premium, designer-looking switch plates at prices well below the European brands. In Bangalore's apartment and villa market, Greatwhite boards have become a common sight in new interiors.

Company snapshot

Greatwhite is an Indian electrical manufacturer focused on switches, wiring accessories and allied home-electrical products. The company has invested heavily in product design and finish quality, and distributes nationally through the standard electrical trade. It is a design-led challenger to the established switch majors rather than a budget alternative.

Greatwhite's electrical product ranges

  • Fiana — the flagship modular range, known for its slim, seamless plate design and wide colour options; the series most buyers mean when they say "Greatwhite switches".
  • Myrah — Greatwhite's bath and allied fittings range, extending the brand beyond the switchboard.
  • Wiring accessories — regulators, dimmers, USB sockets, bells and supporting modules across its ranges.
  • Other lines — Greatwhite also markets MCBs, distribution boards and related products; ask for current availability as the portfolio keeps growing.

What Greatwhite is best at

Greatwhite's edge is design per rupee. A Fiana board delivers a flush, premium look that photographs like a European plate at a distinctly Indian price. It is the natural pick for interiors-led projects — new flats, renovated living rooms, builder show units — where the switchboard is a visible design element. If you want the maximum-safety, maximum-spares choice instead, compare it with Anchor Roma on our price lists; if you want European branding, compare Schneider and Legrand.

How to make sure your Greatwhite product is genuine

  • Check moulding and finish: genuine Fiana plates have precise edges, uniform surface texture and clean rear markings.
  • Verify the range name and module count on the packaging against your bill.
  • Scan the verification QR where provided on the pack.
  • Buy from an authorised channel at market-band prices. The electrical trade runs on 3–5% margins, so a steep discount is a duplicate signal — see our original vs duplicate guide.

Questions, Answered

Greatwhite FAQs

What is Greatwhite Fiana?
Fiana is Greatwhite's flagship modular switch range, known for its slim, seamless plate design and wide colour options. It delivers a premium designer look at a price well below European switch brands.
Is Greatwhite a good switch brand compared to Anchor or Legrand?
Greatwhite competes on design per rupee: better finish than most similarly priced Indian ranges and cheaper than European brands like Legrand or Schneider. Anchor Roma still wins on decade-long spares ubiquity, so choose based on whether design or spares matter more to you.
Are Greatwhite switches expensive?
Greatwhite sits in the mid-to-premium band: above value ranges like Anchor Penta but clearly below imported European series. For an interiors-led project the visual upgrade usually justifies the difference.
