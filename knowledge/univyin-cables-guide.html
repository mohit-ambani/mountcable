Univyin Cables: The Complete Brand Guide

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Univyin Cables is a value-focused wire brand stocked at Mount Cable India for budget-sensitive projects. Not every wiring job needs the most premium label in the market: rental units, site wiring, temporary supplies and cost-driven builds all need honest, standards-marked copper wire at the sharpest workable price — and that is the slot Univyin fills.

Company snapshot

Univyin Cables is an Indian wire and cable manufacturer serving the trade with house wires and flexible cables. It is a trade-driven brand rather than an advertised consumer name: its reputation travels through electricians and dealers who have used it on real jobs. We stock it because it has held up to that test at our counters, and because we can stand behind every coil we sell with our own guarantee of immediate resolution if anything is wrong.

Univyin Cables' electrical product ranges

  • FR house wires — flame-retardant PVC insulated copper wires in the standard 1.0, 1.5, 2.5, 4 and 6 sq mm sizes.
  • Flexible multicore cables — 2-core and 3-core flexes for pumps, appliances and extensions.
  • Project and custom lengths — for contractors, ask about availability of specific put-ups and sizes for bulk work.

What Univyin Cables is best at

Univyin's job is price-to-performance. For a primary residence you plan to live in for decades, we will usually point you to Finolex, Polycab or RR Kabel. But for a rental property, a PG, a shop interior, a temporary site supply or a strict-budget build, Univyin delivers ISI-marked copper wire at a meaningfully lower outlay. The honest rule: match the wire to the life and criticality of the installation, and never save money by undersizing the conductor — a correctly sized value wire beats an undersized premium wire every time.

How to make sure your Univyin Cables product is genuine

  • Check the printed marking along the wire — brand, size and the ISI mark — and the coil label details against your bill.
  • Check the copper at a cut end: bright, clean strands of uniform draw, not dull or brittle wire.
  • Scan the verification QR where provided on the packaging.
  • Buy from an authorised counter. Even for value brands, the 3–5% margin rule applies — a price far below the going rate means the copper or the length is short. Our original vs duplicate guide shows how to check both.

Questions, Answered

Univyin Cables FAQs

Is Univyin a good wire brand?
Univyin is a value-focused Indian wire brand suited to budget-sensitive jobs like rentals, shops and site wiring. Its ISI-marked FR wires deliver honest performance at a lower price than premium labels, and Mount Cable India stands behind every coil it sells.
When should I choose a value wire brand over Finolex or Polycab?
Choose a value brand for rentals, PGs, temporary supplies and strict-budget builds, and a premium brand for a home you will live in long term. Never compromise on conductor size: a correctly sized value wire is safer than an undersized premium wire.
How do I check the quality of a Univyin wire coil?
Check the repeating brand, size and ISI print along the wire, inspect the copper at a cut end for bright uniform strands, and scan the verification QR where provided. Buy at market-band prices from an authorised counter.
