Polycab: The Complete Brand Guide

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Polycab is the largest wires and cables company in India by sales, and over the last decade it has grown into a full electrical brand — fans, switches, lighting, pumps and conduit alongside its core cable business. For a home builder, that means you can specify a large part of your electrical bill of materials from one brand.

Company snapshot

Polycab India is a listed Indian company with large manufacturing capacity in Gujarat and a nationwide distribution network. It began as a cables business and later expanded into FMEG (fast-moving electrical goods). In the Bangalore market, Polycab wire is one of the two or three names every electrician quotes alongside Finolex and RR Kabel.

Polycab's electrical product ranges

  • Polycab FR house wire — flame-retardant copper wire in standard sizes from 1.0 to 6 sq mm, the workhorse for home wiring.
  • Polycab FRLS / low-smoke wire — flame retardant low smoke insulation for flats, basements and commercial interiors.
  • Green Wire — Polycab's more premium, eco-positioned house wire line.
  • LT power and flexible cables — multicore flexes, submersible cables and armoured cables for larger jobs.
  • FMEG — ceiling fans, switches, LED lighting and conduit pipes, useful when you want one-brand consistency.

What Polycab is best at

Polycab's strength is range and availability: from a single 1.5 sq mm coil to armoured cable for a borewell panel, the brand covers it with consistent quality. Pricing is in the mainstream-premium band, generally comparable to Finolex. If your project needs both house wire and heavier cable, Polycab lets you standardise on one brand. For fans and lighting, treat Polycab as a solid value option rather than a designer label — compare finishes in person or against our Polycab brand page.

How to make sure your Polycab product is genuine

  • Genuine coils have sharp, evenly spaced printing on the wire — brand, size and ISI mark — and clean carton printing.
  • Scan the verification QR where provided on the packaging and check that the product details match what you bought.
  • Buy from an authorised channel. Electrical is a 3–5% margin trade, so a Polycab coil at a steep discount is a warning sign, not a bargain. Our original vs duplicate guide explains the price-band logic.
  • For fans and FMEG, check the serial and warranty card — genuine stock always carries proper warranty paperwork.

Questions, Answered

Polycab FAQs

Is Polycab wire as good as Finolex?
Yes, Polycab and Finolex are both premium-mainstream house wire brands with comparable quality and pricing, and electricians in Bangalore routinely accept either. Choose based on price on the day and availability of the sizes you need.
What is the difference between Polycab FR and FRLS wire?
FR wire resists catching fire, while FRLS adds low-smoke insulation that emits less dense smoke if it burns. FRLS is worth the small premium in apartments, basements and any enclosed space where smoke would block escape routes.
How do I verify a genuine Polycab product?
Check the clean repeating print on the wire and carton, scan the verification QR where provided, and buy from an authorised dealer at market-band prices. A discount far beyond 3-5% off market rate usually signals duplicate stock.
