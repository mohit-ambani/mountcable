HPL: The Complete Brand Guide

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

HPL is an Indian electrical manufacturer best known in the trade for switchgear and energy meters. While it advertises less than the consumer-facing giants, electricians and contractors know HPL as a dependable, value-priced source of MCBs, distribution boards and wiring accessories — the unglamorous products that quietly protect a building.

Company snapshot

HPL Electric & Power is a Delhi-headquartered, listed Indian company with decades of manufacturing history. It is one of India's significant makers of energy meters — supplying utilities as well as the retail market — and produces a broad range of low-voltage switchgear, wires, lighting and modular switches from its Indian plants.

HPL's electrical product ranges

  • MCBs, RCCBs and isolators — the core retail range: protection devices in standard ratings for home distribution boards.
  • Distribution boards — SPN and TPN boards in common configurations for flats and independent houses.
  • Energy meters — single-phase and three-phase meters, a category where HPL supplies utilities at scale.
  • Wires and cables — FR house wires in standard sizes.
  • Modular switches and lighting — functional ranges positioned on value rather than designer aesthetics.

What HPL is best at

HPL's sweet spot is protection and metering at honest prices. For a rental property, a site office, a PG building or a budget-conscious home, an HPL MCB and DB package delivers standards-compliant protection at a meaningful saving versus European brands. Its utility-scale meter business also signals real manufacturing depth — companies that pass utility procurement testing know how to build consistent product. How to make sure your HPL product is genuine

  • Check the rating print on the MCB face — crisp, complete and matching the packaging.
  • Check carton print quality and that the model and rating on the box match the device inside.
  • Scan the verification QR where provided on the pack.
  • Buy from an authorised channel at market-band prices. With 3–5% trade margins, an unusually cheap lot of "HPL" switchgear is a duplicate signal — our original vs duplicate guide explains why fake protection gear is the most dangerous counterfeit of all.

Questions, Answered

HPL FAQs

Is HPL a good brand for MCBs?
Yes. HPL is an established Indian switchgear manufacturer whose MCBs and RCCBs meet the required standards at value prices, making it a sensible choice for budget-conscious homes, rentals and site work.
What is HPL best known for?
In the trade, HPL is best known for low-voltage switchgear and energy meters — it supplies meters to utilities at scale — along with distribution boards, wires, switches and lighting.
HPL vs Schneider MCB: which should I buy?
Both are standards-compliant; Schneider carries a premium for its global engineering reputation while HPL delivers dependable protection at a lower price. For a primary residence many buyers choose the premium brand, while HPL is excellent value for rentals and secondary applications.
