Legrand: The Complete Brand Guide

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Legrand is a global specialist in switches, sockets and wiring devices — it is not a conglomerate that happens to make switches, it is a company built around them. In India, Legrand's ranges are a fixture in architect-specified homes, and its protection gear shares shelf space with Schneider at the premium end of the trade.

Company snapshot

Legrand is a French multinational headquartered in Limoges with well over a century of history in electrical wiring devices, selling in a large number of countries. In India it operates across switches, protection devices, cable management and home automation, and distributes through the standard electrical channel with a strong presence in specification-driven projects.

Legrand's electrical product ranges

  • Myrius — the mainstream modular range in India: clean styling, broad module ecosystem, and the series most homes actually install.
  • Arteor — the premium international range with square and round module aesthetics and deep home-automation options; common in high-end villas and hotels.
  • MCBs, RCCBs and distribution boards — premium protection gear trusted by electricians and consultants.
  • Cable management and accessories — trunking, floor boxes and supporting devices, useful in offices and larger homes.

What Legrand is best at

Legrand's strength is the combination of design pedigree and a deep module ecosystem: dimmers, USB chargers, motion sensors and automation modules that click into the same plates. Myrius gives a refined European look at a price competitive with premium Indian ranges, while Arteor plays in true luxury territory. If your interiors budget is tighter, compare Myrius against Greatwhite Fiana and Anchor Roma on our price lists — the right answer depends on the look you want per rupee.

How to make sure your Legrand product is genuine

  • Check moulding precision, uniform plate colour and crisp rear markings; premium brands are precisely the ones counterfeiters target.
  • On MCBs, check the clean rating print on the face and quality packaging.
  • Scan the verification QR where provided on the pack.
  • Buy from an authorised channel at market-band prices. The electrical trade runs on 3–5% margins, so a steep discount on Legrand stock is a duplicate signal — see our original vs duplicate guide.

Questions, Answered

Legrand FAQs

What is the difference between Legrand Myrius and Arteor?
Myrius is Legrand's mainstream modular range for Indian homes with clean European styling at a premium-but-accessible price, while Arteor is the luxury international range with square and round aesthetics and deep home-automation modules.
Is Legrand better than Anchor or Greatwhite?
Legrand offers a deeper premium module ecosystem (dimmers, sensors, automation) and European design pedigree, at a higher price. Anchor wins on spares ubiquity and Greatwhite on design per rupee, so the best choice depends on budget and the look you want.
Does Legrand make MCBs and distribution boards?
Yes. Legrand manufactures premium MCBs, RCCBs and distribution boards that are widely specified by electricians and consultants, alongside its switch and wiring-device ranges.
