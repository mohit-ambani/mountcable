KEI: The Complete Brand Guide

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

KEI Industries is one of India's major cable manufacturers, with deep roots in industrial and institutional projects — the kind of buyer who tests cable before paying for it. That engineering-first background is exactly why KEI house wire is a smart pick for homeowners who care about what is inside the insulation.

Company snapshot

KEI Industries is a Delhi-headquartered, listed Indian company that manufactures a full spectrum of cables — from extra-high-voltage and industrial power cables down to the flexible house wire sold over the counter. Much of its business comes from projects and institutions, and the retail house-wire range benefits from the same plants and quality systems.

KEI's electrical product ranges

  • KEI FR house wire — flame-retardant copper wire in the standard 1.0 to 6 sq mm sizes for home wiring.
  • KEI FRLS / low-smoke house wire — for apartments and enclosed spaces where smoke behaviour matters.
  • Multicore flexible cables — appliance and pump connections.
  • LT/HT power and armoured cables — where KEI's industrial pedigree shows; relevant if your project includes a panel, lift or borewell feed.

If you are unsure which series a dealer is quoting, ask for the exact catalogue name and size — a genuine KEI pack states both clearly.

What KEI is best at

KEI's core strength is conductor and insulation quality at a price that often lands slightly sharper than the most-advertised brands. It spends less on celebrity marketing and more on manufacturing, which makes it attractive for buyers comparing on substance. For a villa or independent house where the same order includes house wire and armoured cable, KEI covers the whole list from one brand. Compare it against Finolex and Polycab on our price lists — KEI frequently wins on value.

How to make sure your KEI product is genuine

  • Check the printing along the wire: brand, size, and ISI mark should repeat cleanly and consistently.
  • Examine carton print quality and check that coil length and size on the label match what you ordered.
  • Scan the verification QR where provided on the packaging.
  • Buy from an authorised channel at market-band prices. Electrical distribution runs on 3–5% margins, so an unusually cheap "KEI" coil is the classic duplicate signal — see our original vs duplicate guide.

KEI FAQs

Is KEI wire good for house wiring?
Yes. KEI is a major Indian cable manufacturer with strong industrial credentials, and its FR and FRLS house wires meet the same standards as other premium brands, often at a slightly sharper price.
Is KEI cheaper than Finolex and Polycab?
KEI often prices marginally below the most-advertised premium brands because it spends less on consumer marketing. The gap is small, so compare quotes on the day for your exact sizes.
How can I tell genuine KEI wire from a duplicate?
Look for clean, repeating brand and ISI printing on the wire, matching details on the carton, and scan the verification QR where provided. Buy from an authorised dealer and avoid steep-discount offers, which usually indicate duplicate stock.
