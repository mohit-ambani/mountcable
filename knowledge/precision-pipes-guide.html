Precision Pipes: The Complete Brand Guide

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Precision Pipes is a conduit and PVC pipe brand that the Bangalore electrical trade relies on for the least visible but most permanent part of a wiring job: the conduits buried in your slab and walls. Wires can be replaced by pulling new ones through the pipe — but only if the pipe itself was good enough to survive concreting and stay smooth inside.

Company snapshot

Precision Pipes is an Indian manufacturer of rigid PVC electrical conduits and related fittings supplied through the electrical trade. Rather than consumer advertising, the brand's reputation is built counter-by-counter with electricians and contractors who judge a conduit by how it bends, how it survives a slab pour and how easily wire pulls through it years later.

Precision Pipes' electrical product ranges

  • Rigid PVC conduits — the standard 20 mm and 25 mm electrical conduits used for concealed wiring in slabs and walls, in medium and heavy grades.
  • Conduit fittings — bends, couplers, junction boxes and accessories that complete a concealed wiring layout.
  • Casing and capping — for surface wiring in retrofits where chasing walls is not practical.
  • Larger PVC pipe sizes — for service entries and multi-cable runs; ask for current availability.

What Precision Pipes is best at

The brand's job is simple: pipes that do not crack when concrete is poured over them, do not collapse at bends, and keep a smooth bore so re-wiring is possible a decade later. Conduit is a small percentage of your electrical budget, yet a crushed or burr-filled cheap pipe can make a future wire replacement impossible without breaking walls. Specify medium or heavy grade for slabs, and insist your contractor uses proper bends rather than heating and kinking the pipe. Conduit pairs with the wire order itself — plan both together using our price lists.

How to make sure your Precision Pipes product is genuine

  • Check the brand and size marking printed along the pipe — clean, repeating print is the first authenticity sign.
  • Check wall thickness and rigidity; a genuine medium/heavy pipe resists finger pressure and does not oval easily.
  • Scan the verification QR where provided on packing or bundles.
  • Buy from an authorised channel at market-band prices — with 3–5% trade margins, suspiciously cheap conduit means thinner walls or reground material. Our original vs duplicate guide covers the checks.

Questions, Answered

Precision Pipes FAQs

What size conduit is used for house wiring?
Concealed house wiring in India typically uses 20 mm rigid PVC conduit for most runs and 25 mm where more wires share a route, in medium or heavy grade for slab work. Your electrician's layout decides the exact mix.
Why does conduit quality matter if it is hidden in the wall?
Because conduit is the only part you can never replace. A quality pipe survives the concrete pour and keeps a smooth bore so future rewiring is a simple pull-through, while a cheap pipe cracks or collapses and can force you to break walls later.
How do I check genuine Precision Pipes conduit?
Check the clean repeating brand and size print along the pipe, test its rigidity and wall thickness, and scan the verification QR where provided. Buy from an authorised dealer at market-band prices rather than chasing the cheapest bundle.
