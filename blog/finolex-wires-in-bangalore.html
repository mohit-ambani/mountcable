What "Finolex wires" means when you are wiring a house

Finolex makes several house-wire ranges, and the difference between them is the thing most first-time buyers miss. For a home in Bangalore you will normally be choosing between:

RangeCoil lengthWhat it is for
Finolex 90M Silver (FR)90 mThe standard flame-retardant house wire; the range most homes are wired with
Finolex 90M Gold (FR)90 mPremium tier with a thicker, tougher insulation and a higher temperature rating
Finolex FRLS (Flamegard)90 m / 300 mLow-smoke insulation for stairwells, basements and anywhere smoke is the bigger hazard
Finolex Ultra90 mThe top of the range for premium builds
Project packing180 m / 300 mLonger coils for builders and contractors, priced per metre

Sizes run from 1.0 sq mm for lighting through 1.5, 2.5, 4 and 6 sq mm for power, AC, geyser and mains circuits. If you are unsure which size a circuit needs, the free wire size calculator works it out with the safety margin already built in. The Silver vs Gold guide covers the range choice.

What Finolex wire costs in Bangalore right now

Finolex prices move with copper, so any figure printed in a blog goes stale. We keep our net prices for every range and size on the buy Finolex wires page and update them whenever the list changes. Taxes are extra as applicable. Two useful reference points:

  • Genuine branded wire runs on a 3 to 5% dealer margin. A quote 15% or more below the market is not a discount. It is paid for by short copper, a short coil or a counterfeit. The price test explains why.

Where to buy Finolex in Bangalore: three kinds of seller

  1. Authorised distributors. Buy directly from Finolex, hold the authorisation certificate, carry every range in depth and can trace any carton back to the company. This is what Mount Cable is.
  2. Authorised dealers. Buy from a distributor, hold a dealer certificate, and are a perfectly good place to buy if the certificate is current and you can scan before paying.
  3. Shops that stock the brand. The majority. They may be buying from a distributor, from another shop, from a wholesale market, or from a person with a van. The carton looks identical in all four cases.

The word "dealer" on a signboard belongs to whoever painted it. The certificate is the only thing that makes it true, and the authorised dealer guide shows what one looks like.

The check that settles it, wherever you buy

Every Finolex carton carries two QR codes. The outer code is printed on the carton label next to the size, grade, length and batch number; scanning it opens Finolex's own verification page and confirms the carton is registered. The inner code is inside the box and can only be reached after opening it; it confirms the contents are what Finolex packed. Both must pass. A genuine carton can be emptied and refilled, in which case the outer code still verifies and only the inner code fails. Do both scans at your own site, before paying.
