House Wiring Cost in Bangalore (2026): What a 2BHK, 3BHK and Villa Actually Cost

By Mount Cable India · July 22, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

"What does house wiring cost in Bangalore?" is one of the most common questions we get on WhatsApp, and it rarely has a single number as an answer. Total wiring cost depends on far more than square footage — it depends on how the home is planned, wired and finished. This guide explains what actually drives the number, gives illustrative planning ranges by home size, and shows how to get an exact figure for your own home.

What actually drives the total cost

  • Built-up area. Larger homes need more wire run length and more points, so cost scales roughly with area — but not perfectly, since layout matters as much as size.
  • Number of points. A home with generous lighting, switch and socket points per room will always cost more in wire and accessories than one wired to a bare minimum.
  • Wire gauge mix. Lighting circuits typically use thinner wire than AC or geyser circuits. A home with more high-load points needs a heavier gauge mix, which costs more per metre.
  • Switch and MCB brand tier. A premium modular switch range and a branded MCB panel cost meaningfully more than an economical range — see our building checklist for how to plan this out room by room.
  • Labour. Conduit laying, point wiring and finishing labour varies by contractor and by how much rework the layout demands.

Material versus labour

Material typically makes up a meaningful share of the total electrical cost, with labour, conduit, boxes and fittings adding to it on top. The exact split varies by project — a home with premium switch brands and heavier gauge wire will see material carry more of the total, while a simply specified home will see labour form a larger share. This is exactly why a single "cost per square foot" figure quoted casually is rarely reliable — it depends on the specification you actually choose.
