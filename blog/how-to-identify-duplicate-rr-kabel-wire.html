How to Identify Duplicate RR Kabel Wire: 7 Checks Before You Pay

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

RR Kabel has spent decades building a reputation for flame-retardant, long-life house wire, and that reputation is exactly what counterfeiters trade on. Wire is the perfect product to fake: once it goes inside a conduit and behind plaster, nobody ever sees it again, and very few buyers can judge copper quality by eye. A duplicate coil looks almost identical on the shelf but may carry thinner copper, recycled metal or substandard insulation that fails years later, hidden inside your walls.

Seven checks before you pay

  • Read the print along the wire. Genuine RR Kabel wire carries crisp, evenly spaced printing along its length: the brand name, the size in sq mm, the applicable standard or ISI mark, and sequential metre marking. Faded, smudged or irregular printing is a warning sign.
  • Check the metre marking runs in sequence. Pull out a few metres and confirm the numbers increase steadily. Fakes often repeat numbers or skip them.
  • Inspect the packaging print quality. Genuine coils have sharp label printing and correct spelling. Counterfeit packaging frequently has blurry logos, wrong fonts or small spelling errors.
  • Use the brand's verification where provided. Where RR Kabel provides a QR code, hologram or scratch-based verification on the pack, use it at the counter before paying. A seller who discourages you from verifying is telling you something.
  • Weigh the coil mentally. A 90 m coil of a given size has a fairly consistent weight across genuine stock. A coil that feels distinctly light deserves suspicion.
  • Look at the copper at the cut end. Genuine copper is bright and uniform. Dull, greyish or uneven strands suggest recycled metal.
  • Check the seal and coil winding. Factory winding is tight and uniform; rewound coils look loose and untidy.

The price test

Electrical distribution runs on 3 to 5 percent margins. No genuine seller can price RR Kabel far below the market and survive, so a price that looks like a steal is the classic duplicate signature. Compare quotes from two or three shops; the outlier that is dramatically cheaper is the one to question, not celebrate.

The channel test

Buy from authorised dealers and distributors. Ask to see the dealer's authorisation certificate, and insist on a proper GST bill that names RR Kabel and the exact item. Counterfeit stock almost always travels through informal, bill-free channels. Our guide on the electrician-retailer nexus behind duplicate wires explains how fakes reach honest customers.
