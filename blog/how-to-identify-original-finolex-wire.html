Safety

How to Identify 100% Original Finolex Wire (and Avoid Fakes)

By Mount Cable India · June 2, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Wire is hidden inside your walls for decades — so buying genuine, quality wire is one of the most important safety decisions in your home. Counterfeit and sub-standard wire uses less or impure copper and weaker insulation, which can overheat. Here's how to protect yourself.

1. Buy from an authorized distributor

The single best safeguard. Authorized distributors source directly through Finolex's official channel, so the product is guaranteed genuine and warranty-backed. Mount Cable India is one of India's largest Finolex distributors — our surety is 100% original Finolex, every time.

2. Check the packaging and printing

Genuine Finolex boxes are cleanly printed with consistent branding, batch details and grade markings (FR, FR-LSH, etc.). Be wary of blurry print, spelling errors or tampered seals.

3. Look for standards markings on the wire

Original wire carries clear, repeated printing along its length — brand, size (sq mm), grade and standards mark. The print should be sharp and evenly spaced.

4. Sequential metre marking

Quality wire is printed with running metre markings so you can verify coil length. Missing or irregular markings are a red flag.

5. Don't chase the lowest price

If a price looks far below the market, ask why. Genuine wire has a real copper cost. A distributor rate from an authorized seller is the right kind of saving — not a suspiciously cheap "deal".
