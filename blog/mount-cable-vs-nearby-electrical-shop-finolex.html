Mount Cable vs Your Nearby Electrical Shop: Who Should Supply Your Finolex Wire?

By Mount Cable India · September 5, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

For a switch, a holder or a length of conduit, the electrical shop near your site is the right place to buy. For Finolex wire, the right seller is whoever can show a Finolex authorisation, let you scan both QR codes on every carton before you pay, give you a GST invoice in your own name and quote a published price. Some nearby shops can do all four. Mount Cable India, an authorised Finolex distributor with free next-day delivery across Bangalore, does all four as standard, which is why distance is no longer a reason to choose one over the other.

What the nearby shop is genuinely good at

  • Speed for small items. The electrician walks over, buys the holder, walks back.
  • Odd sizes and bits. A junction box, a clip, a single MCB.
  • Credit and familiarity. The shop knows the electrician and will run a tab.

We say this plainly because the comparison is only useful if it is honest. The nearby shop earns its place on every site. The question is whether it should also supply the wire.

The commission question, asked fairly

Most nearby shops pay electricians and contractors a percentage of what they bring in. That is how the trade has worked for decades and it is not fraud in itself. But it means that when the electrician says "I will get the wire from my regular shop", the recommendation has a price attached that you cannot see. On wire, where a duplicate coil carries several times the margin of a genuine one, the incentive is worth understanding. Choose the wire seller yourself, ask the electrician to do the wiring, and pay the electrician properly for that. The contractor fraud guide goes into the mechanics.

How to use the nearby shop for wire, if you want to

Plenty of neighbourhood shops are honest and some hold a genuine dealer certificate. If yours does, the test is simple and takes two minutes:

  1. Ask for the Finolex certificate and photograph it.
  2. Ask to scan the outer QR on every carton and the inner QR on one, before paying.
  3. Ask for a bill in your name that names the range and size.
  4. Compare the price with ours. If it is close, buy local with confidence. If it is far below, you know why.

We would rather you bought genuine Finolex from a good shop near you than duplicate wire from anyone. The earlier comparison covers the whole electrical list; this one is about wire because wire is where the difference is invisible and permanent.

The check that settles it, wherever you buy

Every Finolex carton carries two QR codes. The outer code is printed on the carton label next to the size, grade, length and batch number; scanning it opens Finolex's own verification page and confirms the carton is registered. The inner code is inside the box and can only be reached after opening it; it confirms the contents are what Finolex packed. Both must pass. A genuine carton can be emptied and refilled, in which case the outer code still verifies and only the inner code fails. Do both scans at your own site, before paying. Frequently asked questions

Should I buy Finolex wire from the electrical shop near my site?
Only if it can show a Finolex authorisation, let you scan both QR codes before paying, give a bill in your name naming the range and size, and quote a price close to a published reference. If it can, buy local with confidence. If not, have it delivered from an authorised distributor.
What is the nearby electrical shop good for?
Small, low-risk items needed quickly: switches, holders, conduit, clips, a single MCB. Wire is different because you cannot see copper through insulation and the coil is concealed for decades.
Why does my electrician want to buy wire from a particular shop?
Shops commonly pay electricians a commission on purchases they bring. It is normal in the trade, but it means the recommendation carries a hidden cost. Choose the wire seller yourself and pay the electrician properly for the wiring.
Is Mount Cable cheaper than a local electrical shop for Finolex?
Mount Cable sells at distributor prices, published on its buy page. An honest local shop will usually be close to that. A shop that is far cheaper is not competing on price; it is selling something that is not what the label says.
Does Mount Cable deliver to my area for free?
Yes, anywhere in Bangalore, next day and often same day, with payment collected after you have scanned every carton at the site.
Can a local shop be an authorised Finolex dealer?
Yes. Finolex authorises dealers who buy through its distributors. Ask to see the current certificate; a signboard or branded banner is not one.
