Is That V-Guard Wire Genuine? A Buyer's Checklist

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Would you notice if the V-Guard coil handed across the counter was a fake? Most buyers would not, and counterfeiters know it. V-Guard has earned deep trust in South Indian households, which makes its name valuable to copy — and because wire vanishes into walls the day it is installed, a duplicate can hide for years before it fails. Substandard copper runs hot, insulation degrades, and by then the seller is untraceable.

What genuine V-Guard wire looks like

Every legitimate coil gives you several things to verify before money changes hands:

  • Continuous printing on the insulation — the V-Guard name, wire size in sq mm, and the ISI or standards mark, printed sharply and at regular intervals along the length.
  • Sequential metre marking — numbers that climb steadily as the wire is drawn out. Repeated or missing numbers are a classic fake tell, and they also expose short-length coils.
  • Clean packaging — correct spelling everywhere, sharp logo printing, and colours that match what you have seen before. Counterfeit cartons are usually a shade off.
  • Brand verification where provided — if the pack carries a hologram, QR code or scratch-verification feature from V-Guard, use it at the counter, not after you reach home.
  • Bright, uniform copper at the cut end. Dull or greyish strands point to recycled metal.

Two tests that need no technical eye

The price test

Electrical is a 3 to 5 percent margin business. That single fact is your best protection: a genuine dealer cannot sell V-Guard far below market and stay in business, so an unbelievably low price is the surest counterfeiting signature there is. Collect quotes from two or three shops. Respect the consensus price; interrogate the outlier.

The channel test

Buy from authorised dealers or distributors, ask to see their authorisation certificate, and insist on a GST bill naming V-Guard and the specific item. The paper trail is what protects your warranty and your insurance claim. Our explainer on checking dealer authorisation shows what a genuine certificate looks like — the same logic applies to every brand.

Frequently asked questions

How do I verify V-Guard wire is genuine?
Insist on sealed cartons, check print sharpness and alignment on the label, confirm size, grade, length and batch are printed rather than stickered, scan the QR where available, and look at the repeating markings along the insulation for even spacing.
What is the most common giveaway on duplicate V-Guard wire?
Insulation printing that is faint, uneven or incorrectly spaced, combined with a carton whose print quality does not match the rest of the packaging. Copied artwork loses definition each time it is reprinted.
Should I weigh the coil?
A significantly light coil suggests copper shortfall or short length, and experienced buyers do check. It is a useful cross-check rather than a definitive test, since packaging weight varies — the more reliable route is a sealed carton from an authorised source.
Is V-Guard wire cheaper than other major brands?
V-Guard often sits slightly below Finolex and Polycab, but the honest spread between major brands on comparable grades is single-digit percent. A quote far below that spread indicates a different product rather than a cheaper brand.
