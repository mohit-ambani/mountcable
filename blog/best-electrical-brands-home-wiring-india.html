Frequently asked questions

Which electrical brands are best for home wiring in India?
For wires, the established names all use electrolytic-grade copper to the same IS standards and differ mainly in premium insulation ranges and local availability. For switchgear and switches the differences are larger, and matching the distribution board and MCBs to one brand matters more than the brand chosen.
Should I use the same brand for wires, switches and switchgear?
Not necessarily. Wires, modular switches and switchgear are separate decisions and few manufacturers lead in all three. What does matter is keeping the distribution board and its MCBs within one brand family, and keeping switch modules and plates within one series.
How do I avoid counterfeits across all these categories?
Buy from an authorised dealer for each brand, insist on sealed packaging, take a GST invoice naming brand, series and specification for every line, and verify by QR at your site before paying. The purchase channel does more work than any visual check.
Where is it worth spending more?
Switchgear first, because an MCB or RCCB either works during a fault or does not. Then wire grade and correct sizing. Modular switches are the category where a modest choice is entirely reasonable if the budget is tight.
