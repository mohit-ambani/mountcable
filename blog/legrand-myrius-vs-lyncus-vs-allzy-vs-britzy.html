Legrand Myrius vs Lyncus vs Allzy vs Britzy: Which Switch Range for Your Home

By Mount Cable India · September 8, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Britzy and Allzy are Legrand's entry ranges at the same mechanism price, with Allzy adding coloured plates; Lyncus is the mid range with the widest plate palette and the most modern modules; Myrius is the premium range with glass, metallic and antibacterial options. For most Bangalore homes, Lyncus through the house with Myrius in the living room is the combination that balances looks and budget.

Every range prices the mechanism, the cover plate with support frame and the flush box separately. The full lists are on the Legrand switches price list.

Britzy

The everyday range: white mechanisms, white plates from 1 to 18 modules, a 6A switch at ₹56 MRP and a 6/16A socket at ₹294. Fan regulators, an 8-step regulator, RJ45 and TV sockets and hotel functions are all there. What it lacks is plate finishes; Britzy is white. It carries the same 10-year replacement policy on switches and sockets as Myrius.

Allzy

Allzy matches Britzy on the 6A switch (₹56) and adds a 10A switch line at ₹60, a BLDC fan regulator at ₹938, and plates in white, rose gold, gold, matt black and aluminium champagne. Matt black mechanisms cost about 15 to 20 percent more than white. For a budget build that still wants a black or metallic plate in the hall, Allzy is the answer.

Lyncus

Lyncus is where most of our Legrand switch orders land. The 6A switch is ₹100, the 6/16A socket ₹434, the fan regulator ₹734, and the modules include USB-C chargers at 20, 30, 45 and 65 W, a Bluetooth sound receiver, a BLDC regulator and a rotary LED dimmer. Plates come in classic white, chic grey, brushed silver, alu sunset and champagne, from ₹184 to ₹974 for white and a little more for the metallics. Mylinc, with its white dove and matt grey mechanisms, sits alongside it.

Myrius

The premium range: the 6A switch is ₹180, the socket ₹684, and the plates run from ₹306 for white to ₹1,498 for 18 module, with charcoal grey, ice white, ice black, pearl champagne, sonic silver and 3 mm toughened glass above that. Myrius adds the antibacterial series with silver-ion surfaces, a modular MCB that sits in the plate, the full hospitality set, and the IoT and connected modules. It is what architects specify for premium apartments and villas.

What is common to all four

The mechanisms are Legrand's own, ISI-marked where the standard applies, with the same 230 V ratings and the same Legrand support. The metal flush boxes are shared across ranges. And the price test is the same: a plate or module far below the MRP above is not the Legrand product. Frequently asked questions

Which Legrand switch range is the cheapest?
Britzy and Allzy, both at ₹56 MRP for a 6A one-way switch. Allzy adds coloured plates and a BLDC regulator; Britzy is white only.
Is Legrand Lyncus better than Myrius?
Myrius is the premium range with more plate finishes, glass, the antibacterial series and automation modules. Lyncus offers the most modern modules at a mid price. Lyncus is better value; Myrius is better looking and more specified.
What is the difference between Legrand Allzy and Britzy?
Same mechanism price on the 6A switch. Allzy adds a 10A switch line, a BLDC fan regulator and plates in rose gold, gold, matt black and aluminium champagne; Britzy is white only.
Can I mix Legrand ranges in one house?
Yes, and most homes do: Lyncus or Allzy through the house and Myrius plates in the living room. Boxes are shared; plates and mechanisms must match within a board.
Which Legrand range do architects specify?
Myrius for premium residences and Arteor or Arteor Advance for luxury and hospitality projects. Lyncus is increasingly specified for mid-range apartments.
