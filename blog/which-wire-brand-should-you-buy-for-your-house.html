Which Wire Brand Should You Buy for Your House? Finolex, Polycab and Every Other Option Compared

By Mount Cable India · July 23, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Ask around before wiring a new home in Bangalore and you will hear almost every brand in the market mentioned at some point — Finolex, Polycab, Havells, V-Guard, Anchor, RR Kabel, KEI, Fybros, Orbit and more. With this many names in circulation, it is easy to end up more confused after asking five people than before you started. This guide gives a straightforward, situation-based answer, rather than a long list of pros and cons for every brand on the market.

For most homes, Finolex is the recommendation

Across the large majority of home-wiring decisions this guide covers — safety-first buyers, first-time home builders, anyone who simply wants the single most broadly trusted and specified name for FR and FR-LSH house wire — Finolex is the recommendation. The reason is straightforward: Finolex has built a decades-long track record in Indian house wiring and is universally recognised among electricians as a safety benchmark for wire. That recognition matters in practice, because it means every electrician you hire already knows the range, already trusts the FR-LSH rating, and rarely raises a second opinion when you name the brand. Taken together, this covers roughly nine in ten of the buying situations most homeowners actually face.

Where Polycab is the better call

There is one clear situation where Polycab becomes the better recommendation: when you specifically want a single brand to cover wires, fans, lighting and switchgear together under one name — what the industry calls a full FMEG requirement, short for Fast Moving Electrical Goods. Polycab's real differentiator over Finolex is not the wire itself so much as the breadth of its catalogue across categories. Finolex, by contrast, is fundamentally a wires-and-cables specialist, with a narrower footprint outside that core. If your project genuinely benefits from sourcing wires and fittings and fans from one manufacturer's catalogue, Polycab is worth prioritising for that reason alone.

A quick situation-based summary

SituationRecommended brand

Want one brand for wires + fans + lighting + switchgearPolycab
Budget renovation, small jobFinolex

Where the other brands fit in

None of this means Havells, V-Guard, Anchor, RR Kabel, KEI, Fybros or Orbit should be ruled out. Each is an established name in the Indian electrical market and each is worth comparing on its own merits, particularly if you have a specific reason to prefer one — familiarity with an electrician, a particular product range, or a specific project requirement.
