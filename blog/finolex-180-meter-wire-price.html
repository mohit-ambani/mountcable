When the 180 m coil makes sense

The 180 m coil is two standard coils in one length. It is the practical choice for a 2BHK or 3BHK where a size is needed in more than one coil: fewer joints than two 90 m coils, and easier to handle on site than a 300 m coil. It is made in FR and FR-LSH; the Silver and Gold packs are 90 m only.

Before you pay

Buy sealed cartons, scan the outer QR on the label and the inner QR inside the box (Finolex wire scanner), and take a GST invoice naming the range, size and coil length. A long coil sold loose or re-wound has no batch number and no way to verify it.

Is the 180 m coil cheaper per metre than the 90 m coil?
