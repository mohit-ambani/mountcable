Think Your Shop Sold You Duplicate Finolex? Bring It to Mount Cable and We Will Check It, Free

By Mount Cable India · September 5, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

If you suspect the Finolex wire a shop sold you is duplicate, bring a coil and its carton to either Mount Cable showroom in Bengaluru, or send photos on WhatsApp, and we will check it for free. We are an authorised Finolex distributor, we handle genuine stock every day, and we will tell you plainly whether the carton, the codes, the markings and the copper match what Finolex ships. There is no charge and no obligation to buy anything from us.

Why we offer this

Two reasons, and neither is charity. First, every duplicate coil that goes into a Bangalore house damages the brand we have built 35 years of business on, so we would rather it were caught. Second, a person who has seen a duplicate next to a genuine coil once never buys blind again, and those people become our customers without any selling from us. Checking your wire is the most persuasive thing we can do.

Signs that should make you ask

  • The price was well below what the buy Finolex wires page shows for the same range and size.
  • The shop would not let you scan before paying, or scanned it for you on their own phone.
  • The outer QR verified but nobody opened a carton to scan the inner one.
  • The QR did not scan, or scanned to something other than Finolex's own verification page. See QR not scanning.
  • The scan said the code had already been used. See already-scanned codes.
  • The printing along the insulation is uneven, smudged or missing. See insulation markings.
  • The bill says "wire" with a quantity and no range, size or brand.
  • The electrician or contractor chose the shop and is reluctant to say where it is.

One of these is a reason to check. Two or more is a reason to stop the wiring until you have.

  1. Walk in. Chickpete showroom: 10/3 Sri Complex, BVK Iyengar Road, Bengaluru 560053. Jayanagar showroom: Jayanagar, Bengaluru 560011. Bring one coil and its carton, and the bill if you have one.
  2. Or send photos on WhatsApp +91 88676 76700. The carton label, the inner QR, a stretch of the insulation printing, a cut end, and the bill. We can tell you a lot from photos and will say when we cannot.
  3. Or ask us to look at delivery. If you are ordering from us anyway, bring the doubtful coil out when the vehicle arrives and we will check it there.

What we will and will not say

We will tell you whether the coil matches genuine Finolex on each of the checks above, and what to do next. If it is genuine, we will say so; it happens often and we are glad when it does. If it is not, we will tell you which checks failed and give you the evidence in writing so you can act on it. What we will not do is speculate about the shop or name it as a seller of duplicates on the strength of one coil. The complaint guide covers how to take it up with the shop and with Finolex, whose customer care is 1800-209-0166.

If it turns out to be duplicate

Do not let it go into the walls. Photograph everything, keep the bill, and use the complaint guide. If you need replacement wire quickly we can deliver next day and you scan every carton before paying, but that is your decision and it is not a condition of the check.

Cross-check us. Send your list to WhatsApp 88676 76700 for an itemised quote within 60 minutes and use it as your reference price anywhere in Bangalore. There is no obligation to buy from us — plenty of people ask purely to check someone else.

Frequently asked questions

Will Mount Cable check whether my Finolex wire is genuine?
Yes, free, for anyone in Bangalore. Bring a coil and its carton to the Chickpete or Jayanagar showroom, or send photos on WhatsApp 88676 76700. We compare it against genuine stock and tell you what we find, with no obligation to buy.
What signs suggest a shop sold me duplicate Finolex?
A price well below the published rate, a shop that would not let you scan before paying, an outer QR that verified without anyone scanning the inner one, a code that would not scan or had already been used, uneven insulation printing, and a bill that says only 'wire' without range or size.
What does Mount Cable check on a suspect coil?
The outer and inner QR codes, the carton print and batch details, the insulation markings against a genuine coil of the same range, the strand count and copper at a cut end, coil length and weight, and the price on the bill.
Can I get Finolex wire checked from photos?
Often, yes. Send the carton label, the inner QR, a stretch of the insulation printing, a cut end and the bill on WhatsApp. We will say clearly when the photos are not enough and a physical check is needed.
What should I do if the wire turns out to be duplicate?
Do not install it. Keep the bill, photograph the carton and codes, and follow the complaint route with the shop and with Finolex customer care on 1800-209-0166. Mount Cable will give you its findings in writing to support the complaint.
Does Mount Cable charge for checking wire bought elsewhere?
No. The check is free and there is no obligation to buy replacement wire from us.
