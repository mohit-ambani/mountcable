RR Kabel vs Polycab vs Finolex: Which Wire Brand to Buy in 2026

By Mount Cable India · September 8, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Finolex is the value-and-verification choice, Polycab the widest range with the most grades, and RR Kabel the premium brand. On the copper all three are ISI 694 and any of them wires a house for life. Choose Finolex for a home where price and a clean QR check matter, Polycab when you want FRLF or HR-FR grades or are buying the whole electrical package from one brand, and RR Kabel when the specification or the client asks for the top of the market.

Grades: what each brand calls the same thing

  • Standard FR house wire: Finolex Silver or Gold, Polycab Etira, RR Kabel Superex.
  • Low smoke for flats and stairwells: Finolex Flamegard FR-LSH, Polycab FRLS-H or Primma/Optima FRLF, RR Kabel Firex LS0H.
  • Halogen-free: Finolex Finoultra, Polycab HFFR, RR Kabel Firex LS0H.
  • Heat resistant: Polycab Maxima HR-FR, RR Kabel HR-FR; Finolex covers the same points by sizing up one step in Gold Pack.

The grade matters more than the brand. A flat above the fourth floor should be wired in a low-smoke grade from any of the three; an independent house is fine in standard FR from any of the three. FR vs FR-LSH vs HFFR explains the difference.

Verification and duplicates

All three brands are copied, Finolex and Polycab most because they sell most. Finolex's two-QR check on its own server is the strongest defence: the Finolex wire scanner answers in ten seconds. Polycab and RR Kabel rely on holograms, batch printing and, for RR Kabel, a brand app. Whichever brand you buy, the printing along the wire must match the carton, and a coil offered far below the list prices above is the usual sign of a problem. Frequently asked questions

Which is the best wire brand: RR Kabel, Polycab or Finolex?
All three are ISI 694 copper house wire. Finolex is the value-and-verification choice, Polycab has the widest range of grades, RR Kabel is the premium brand. For most Bangalore homes Finolex; for a one-brand package Polycab; for a specified premium build RR Kabel.
Which is cheaper, Polycab or Finolex?
Coil for coil the lists are close: a 1.5 sq mm 90 m coil lists at ₹3,640 for Finolex Silver against ₹3,820 for Polycab Etira. Dealer prices on both are well below list. Mount Cable India's Finolex Silver 1.5 sq mm is ₹2,257 taxes extra.
Is RR Kabel more expensive than Polycab and Finolex?
Yes. RR Kabel is positioned and priced as the premium brand, above both Polycab and Finolex for the same size and grade.
Which brand is best for a flat?
Any low-smoke grade: Finolex Flamegard FR-LSH, Polycab FRLS-H or FRLF, or RR Kabel Firex LS0H. The grade matters more than the brand.
Which brand is easiest to verify?
Finolex, with two QR codes checked live against Finolex's own server. Polycab and RR Kabel rely on holograms, batch printing and, for RR Kabel, a brand app.
