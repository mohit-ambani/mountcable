Mount Cable vs HomeRun for Finolex Wires: What Actually Arrives in the Box

By Mount Cable India · September 5, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

HomeRun and Mount Cable solve different problems. HomeRun, from ProjectHero, is a quick-commerce platform that delivers construction materials across Bangalore in about 60 minutes with cash on delivery and no minimum order, sourcing from multiple suppliers. Mount Cable India is an authorised Finolex distributor that sells from its own sealed stock, publishes net prices, delivers free next day and takes payment only after you have scanned the outer and inner QR on every carton. If the job has stopped and you need two items now, the platform is the right call. If you are buying the wire for a whole house, the authorised chain is.

What "incorrect product" means in wire, and why it happens

People increasingly bring us coils bought through delivery apps and ask us to check them. The commonest problem is not a counterfeit. It is the wrong product: a Silver coil where Gold was ordered, a 1.5 sq mm coil in a 2.5 listing, a coil from a different range because the one listed was out of stock at the supplier that day. Wire ranges look alike in a photo and a delivery rider is not a wire specialist, so the substitution passes unnoticed until an electrician opens the carton on site.

This is a structural feature of any model that aggregates stock from several suppliers to hit a delivery time. It is not an accusation against any company, and HomeRun's own listing, packing and support may catch it every time. But it is the thing you should check for, and it is the thing a single-source distributor does not have to worry about, because there is one stock, one range list and one person accountable for picking the right carton.

What to check if you order from any app

We would say the same about any multi-supplier platform, and about ourselves:

  1. Before the rider leaves, read the carton label: range (Silver, Gold, FRLS), size in sq mm, and coil length. Match it to what you ordered, not to the brand name alone.
  2. Scan the outer QR on every carton. Open one and scan the inner QR. If the app's flow makes that awkward, that is worth knowing.
  3. Check the price against a published reference. A big gap either way is information.
  4. Keep the invoice and make sure it names the range and size.

If anything fails, do not install it. Bring it to us and we will check it free, whoever sold it.

Frequently asked questions

Is HomeRun or Mount Cable better for Finolex wires?
For a coil needed in the next hour, HomeRun's 60-minute delivery is the right tool. For the wire going into a whole house, Mount Cable's authorised single-source stock, published net prices and scan-before-you-pay delivery are the safer purchase. They solve different problems.
What does 'incorrect product' mean when buying wire online?
Usually the wrong range or size rather than a counterfeit: Silver instead of Gold, 1.5 sq mm instead of 2.5, or a substitute range because the listed one was out of stock at the supplier. Wire ranges look alike in photos, so always read the carton label against your order before the rider leaves.
Can I scan the Finolex QR codes on an app delivery before paying?
It depends on the platform's delivery flow. Try to scan the outer QR on every carton and the inner QR on at least one before the rider leaves. With Mount Cable, scanning both codes before payment is the standard delivery process.
Does HomeRun sell genuine Finolex wire?
This page makes no claim either way about any named company. A multi-supplier platform structurally cannot show one authorisation for the specific carton you receive, so ask for it, read the label, scan both codes and keep the invoice, exactly as you should with any seller including Mount Cable.
What should I do if an app delivered the wrong Finolex range?
Do not install it. Photograph the carton label and your order, use the platform's return process, and if you are unsure whether the coil is genuine bring it to Mount Cable for a free check.
Does Mount Cable deliver as fast as an app?
Not in 60 minutes. Delivery is free across Bangalore next day and often the same day, with every carton scanned at the site before you pay.
