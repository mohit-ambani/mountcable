Finolex Wire Price in Bangalore: How to Buy at Distributor Rate

By Mount Cable India · June 2, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

If you're wiring a house, wire is one of your biggest electrical costs — so it pays to understand how Finolex pricing works and how to buy at the genuine distributor rate.

What affects the price of house wire

  • Copper rate — wire prices move with the global copper market, so they change frequently. Always ask for today's rate.
  • Cross-section (sq mm) — thicker wires use more copper and cost more per coil.
  • Coil length — Finolex sells 90M, 180M and 300M coils; per-metre cost usually improves on longer coils.
  • Grade — FR, FR-LSH and Finolex Ultra are priced differently for their safety features.

MRP vs distributor price

Local shops typically sell near MRP. As one of India's largest Finolex distributors, we pass the distributor rate on to you — the same rate your local shop pays. For a full house, that difference adds up significantly.
