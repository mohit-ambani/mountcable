Finolex Wire Price in Bangalore: How to Buy at Distributor Rate

By Mount Cable India · June 2, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

If you're wiring a house, wire is one of your biggest electrical costs — so it pays to understand how Finolex pricing works and how to buy at the genuine distributor rate.

What affects the price of house wire

  • Copper rate — wire prices move with the global copper market, so they change frequently. Always ask for today's rate.
  • Cross-section (sq mm) — thicker wires use more copper and cost more per coil.
  • Coil length — Finolex sells 90M, 180M and 300M coils; per-metre cost usually improves on longer coils.
  • Grade — FR, FR-LSH and Finolex Ultra are priced differently for their safety features.

MRP vs distributor price

Local shops typically sell near MRP. As one of India's largest Finolex distributors, we pass the distributor rate on to you — the same rate your local shop pays. For a full house, that difference adds up significantly.

Frequently asked questions

What is the price of Finolex wire in Bangalore?
Approximately ₹1,950 to ₹2,250 for a 1.5 sq mm FR 90m coil, ₹3,150 to ₹3,650 for 2.5 sq mm and ₹4,700 to ₹5,400 for 4.0 sq mm in 2026. These are reference bands that move with copper prices, so WhatsApp 88676 76700 for today's exact rate.
How do I buy Finolex wire at distributor rate?
Buy directly from an authorised distributor rather than through a contractor's with-material contract, order the whole house in one go rather than floor by floor, and ask for a written quote with a validity period so the rate is held while you build.
Why do Finolex prices change so often?
Because a wire coil is mostly copper, and copper is an internationally traded commodity priced in dollars. Rates track the copper market and the rupee-dollar exchange rate, with periodic manufacturer MRP revisions resetting the band.
Is a big discount on Finolex wire genuine?
Rarely. Wires run on 3 to 5% dealer margin, so a discount of 15% or more is not a discount but a different product — usually copper shortfall, short coil length or an outright duplicate. Ask to see the carton, the batch and the QR before paying.
