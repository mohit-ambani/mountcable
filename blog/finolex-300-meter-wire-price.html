300 m coil or 90 m coils: which is cheaper?

Per metre, the long coil. In 2.5 sq mm the 300 m FR coil works out to ₹36.2 a metre, against ₹40.9 a metre for the 90 m Silver FR coil. The long coil also means fewer joints. It suits a whole house or a project where you know the quantity; for a small job the 90 m coil avoids leftover wire.

Is there a 300 m coil in 4 and 6 sq mm?

No. In 4.0 and 6.0 sq mm the long coil is 200 m: ₹11,278 for 4.0 sq mm and ₹16,948 for 6.0 sq mm in FR.

Before you pay

Buy sealed cartons, scan the outer QR on the label and the inner QR inside the box (Finolex wire scanner), and take a GST invoice naming the range, size and coil length. A long coil sold loose or re-wound has no batch number and no way to verify it.
