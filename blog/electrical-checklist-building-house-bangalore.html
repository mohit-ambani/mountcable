Electrical Materials Checklist for Building a House in Bangalore

By Mount Cable India · June 2, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Building a home means buying a lot of electrical material. Use this checklist so you order everything in one go and avoid last-minute site delays.

Wires & cables

  • House wires across gauges — 1.0, 1.5, 2.5, 4.0 sq mm (Finolex, Polycab, KEI, RR Kabel)
  • Co-axial cable (TV), telephone cable, and internet/LAN cable
  • Bell wire and flexible multi-core cable for appliances

Switches, sockets & accessories

  • Modular switches and sockets, plates and frames
  • Fan regulators, bell push, USB sockets, TV/data outlets

Protection & distribution

  • Main and sub distribution boards (DBs)
  • MCBs, RCCBs/RCBOs and isolators sized to your load

Pipes, conduits & boxes

  • PVC conduits, bends, couplers and junction boxes
  • Modular mounting boxes and fan hooks/boxes

Lighting

  • LED bulbs, battens, panel and downlights, outdoor/flood lights

Earthing & finishing

  • Earthing wire/strip and accessories
  • 3M insulation tape, connectors, lugs and cable ties
