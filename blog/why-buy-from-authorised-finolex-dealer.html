Buyer Protection

Why Buying from an Authorised Finolex Dealer Should Be Non-Negotiable

By Mount Cable India · September 5, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

Buying Finolex wire from an authorised dealer or distributor should be a fixed rule, not a preference, because authorisation is the only thing that connects the coil in your hand to the company that made it. An authorised seller buys through Finolex's own chain, holds a certificate that can be withdrawn, issues an invoice the manufacturer will honour, and answers to Finolex if a complaint is raised. An unauthorised seller offers none of those, however genuine the carton looks.

What authorisation is, in plain terms

Finolex sells to a limited number of distributors, who sell to dealers and to end customers. Each holds a written authorisation that names the firm, the territory and the period. It is renewed, and it can be cancelled. That single document is the difference between a shop that is inside the supply chain and a shop that is merely selling the brand.

Why the carton alone cannot protect you

People assume a sealed Finolex carton with a QR code is proof enough. It is strong evidence, and you should always scan it, but it is not the same as authorisation, for three reasons:

  1. Cartons get refilled. A genuine, registered carton can be emptied and repacked with duplicate wire. The outer code still verifies. Only the inner code, reachable after opening, tells the truth. Read the inner QR guide.
  2. Codes get cloned. A code copied from a genuine carton scans as genuine until Finolex's system notices it has been scanned too many times, by which point several people have paid for it. See "this code has already been scanned".
  3. Verification is a check, not a remedy. If your scan fails, what happens next depends on who you bought from. An authorised dealer replaces it and takes it up with the company. An unauthorised shop shrugs.

Authorisation is what makes the QR check worth something after it fails.

The margin argument, once more

Branded house wire is a thin-margin business. The distributor earns 3 to 5% and the dealer a little more. There is no room in that structure for a 15% or 20% discount on genuine stock. When a shop offers one, the money is coming from somewhere, and the only places it can come from are less copper, a shorter coil, or a coil that was never Finolex. The authorised chain cannot offer that price, and that is the point: the price you cannot get from an authorised dealer is the price you should not want.

How to check a dealer's claim in two minutes

  1. Ask for the current Finolex authorisation certificate. Look for the firm's name, the period and the signature. Photograph it.
  2. Ask whether you can scan the outer and inner QR on every carton before paying. The answer should be an immediate yes.
  3. Ask for an invoice in your own name that states the range and size. "Wire, 20 coils" is not an invoice that supports a claim.
  4. Compare the quote with a published reference such as the buy Finolex wires page.

Frequently asked questions

Why should I only buy Finolex wire from an authorised dealer?
Because authorisation is the only link between the coil and the manufacturer. An authorised dealer buys through Finolex's own chain, holds a certificate that can be withdrawn, issues an invoice that supports a claim, and answers to Finolex if something is wrong. No other seller offers those protections.
What does a Finolex authorisation certificate look like?
A written authorisation naming the firm, the territory and the validity period, signed by Finolex. Ask to see the current one and photograph it. A signboard, a map listing or a branded banner is not a certificate.
Is a sealed Finolex carton with a QR code enough proof?
It is strong evidence and you should always scan it, but a genuine carton can be refilled and a code can be cloned. The inner QR inside the box catches refilling. Authorisation is what makes the check worth something if it fails, because an authorised dealer replaces the coil and raises it with Finolex.
Can an authorised Finolex dealer give a big discount?
Not on genuine stock. Branded house wire runs on a 3 to 5% distributor margin, so a discount of 15% or more is funded by short copper, a short coil or a counterfeit. The price an authorised dealer cannot offer is the price to avoid.
How do I verify a shop is an authorised Finolex dealer?
Ask for the current certificate, ask whether you can scan both QR codes before paying, ask for an invoice in your name naming the range and size, and compare the quote against a published reference price. All four answers should be yes, immediately.
