Reading the grades. Finolex Silver and Gold are both 90 m coils — Silver is the standard FR line and Gold sits above it. FR-LSH is flame retardant low smoke and halogen, which matters in flats, stairwells and any enclosed escape route, and costs a little more. FR and FR-LSH are also made in 180 m coils, and in a long coil that is 300 m for 1.0–2.5 sq mm and 200 m for 4.0 and 6.0 sq mm. Longer coils work out cheaper per metre and waste less at joints.

What 1.0 sq mm wire is used for

Finolex 1.0 sq mm is the size for light and fan points, and short 6A lighting runs. In a normal house that means ceiling lights, fans, wall lights. The matching protection is a 6A, Curve B breaker at the distribution board.

The most common lighting-circuit size in Indian homes.

Typical requirement: 3–4 coils in a 2BHK, 5–7 in a 3BHK, though the real number depends on your point count and the run length from the distribution board. Work out your own quantity with the wire quantity calculator, and confirm the size for each circuit with the wire size calculator — a long run can need the next size up purely because of voltage drop.

Why the price moves

A wire coil is mostly copper, and copper is an internationally traded commodity priced in dollars. Finolex revises its list periodically as copper and the rupee move, so the figures above are current to 21 May 2026 and will change again. That is also why no honest seller publishes a single fixed rate — the full explanation is in why wire prices keep changing.

Before you pay for any 1.0 sq mm coil
