Which rating goes where in a house

  • 6A or 10A SP: lighting and fan circuits.
  • 16A SP: socket circuits, kitchen points, geyser.
  • 20A or 25A SP: air conditioner on its own circuit.
  • 32A, 40A or 63A DP: the incomer at the distribution board, sized to the sanctioned load.

Match the MCB to the wire, not just the appliance: see wire and MCB for AC and geyser and the MCB selector.

C curve or D curve

Homes use C curve. D curve is for motors and other loads with a high starting current; a D curve 6A to 32A single pole MCB is ₹237 (MRP ₹678). Full tables with SPN and TPN: Legrand MCB price list, RCCB, distribution boards, best price on Legrand MCBs.

Check it is a genuine Legrand MCB

Buy in the sealed Legrand box with a GST invoice, and run the checks in original Legrand MCB: seven checks. A price well below a stockist's net price is the warning sign.
