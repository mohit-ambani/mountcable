Is Your Shop an Authorised Finolex Dealer? Ask for the Certificate

By Mount Cable India · July 21, 2026 · 35 years serving Bengaluru

There's one question that instantly separates genuine Finolex sellers from the rest, and almost nobody asks it: "Are you an authorised Finolex dealer or distributor — and may I see your certificate?"

The certificate every authorised partner holds

Finolex appoints its dealers and distributors formally, and authorised channel partners hold a certificate from Finolex confirming their status. A genuine partner is proud of it — it usually hangs at the counter or is produced within a minute of asking. Hesitation, excuses ("it's with the owner", "it's at the other branch"), or offence at the question are all answers in themselves.

Why authorisation matters so much

  • Supply chain you can trust. Authorised dealers buy through Finolex's official channel, so there's no gap where duplicates can enter. Unauthorised sellers buy from "the market" — and that's exactly where counterfeit stock circulates.
  • Warranty that actually stands. Brand warranty support flows through the authorised channel with proper billing.
  • Accountability. An authorised partner has a relationship with Finolex to protect. A roadside seller has nothing to lose by passing off a fake.

The full 5-point authorised-seller check

  • 1. Ask the question — authorised Finolex dealer or distributor? — and ask to see the certificate.
  • 2. Scan the QR code on the cartons being billed to you. Here's how.
  • 3. Compare the price with 2–3 shops. Genuine sellers all land near the same rate; if someone's price is a steal, it's a duplicate.
  • 4. Insist on sealed boxes — 90M cartons if you're unsure of the shop — never loose or cut wire.
  • 5. Take a GST bill naming the brand, range and sizes. It's your proof and your warranty trail.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if a shop is an authorised dealer?
Ask to see the authorisation certificate for that specific brand, and check that the name on it matches the shop's billing name. Authorised dealers keep it and are happy to show it. Hesitation, an expired certificate or a certificate in a different entity's name are all worth taking seriously.
Does buying from an authorised dealer cost more?
Not meaningfully. Genuine wire is MRP-linked and runs on 3 to 5% margin, so authorised and unauthorised sellers of genuine stock quote within a few percent of each other. A much lower price generally means different stock, not a better deal.
Why does authorisation matter if the product looks the same?
Because it decides what happens when something goes wrong. Warranty claims, replacements and batch traceability all run through the authorised channel. Product bought outside it may be genuine and still leave you with no route to a claim.
What paperwork should I get with my order?
A proper GST invoice in your own name listing the brand, size, grade and quantity of every item. That invoice is what makes a warranty claim possible, and it is also what stops material bought for your house being billed to someone else.
