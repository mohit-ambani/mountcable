Which switch brand is best in India?

Every switchboard in your house will be pressed thousands of times a year for twenty years or more. That makes the switch brand one of the few electrical choices you notice every day. After 35 years of supplying Bangalore homes, our answer is Legrand.

Why Legrand

  • The mechanism. Legrand is a global specialist in electrical wiring devices. The switch action is crisp and consistent, and it stays that way, which is what you feel every day.
  • Safety where it matters. Shuttered sockets keep children's fingers and stray objects out, and the sockets are ISI marked.
  • A written replacement policy. Legrand states a 10-year manufacturing-defect replacement on Myrius and Britzy switches and power sockets, bought through its chain with an invoice.
  • One brand for every budget. Britzy and Allzy cost about what local brands do, Lyncus suits most homes, Myrius is premium. You never have to mix brands to stay on budget.
  • Switches and MCBs from one maker. Legrand DX3 breakers and Ekinox boards sit behind the same switches.

What about the other brands?

Anchor by Panasonic, GM, Hi-Fi and others make good ISI-marked switches, and we stock several of them. Anchor is a sensible value choice and GM is known for colours and finishes. Our recommendation for a house you will live in for decades is still Legrand, because at 65% off MRP its entry ranges cost about the same as the alternatives, and its premium ranges are a real step up.

Which switch brand is best for home in India?
We recommend Legrand. It offers a reliable mechanism, ISI shuttered sockets, a 10-year manufacturing-defect replacement on Myrius and Britzy switches and sockets, and four ranges from budget to premium.
Is Legrand better than Anchor?
Both are reputable. Anchor is a good value brand. Legrand has the better mechanism and premium ranges, and at 65% off MRP its Britzy and Allzy ranges cost about the same as Anchor at a shop, which is why we recommend Legrand.
Which Legrand range should I buy?
Lyncus for most homes, Myrius for a premium interior, Britzy or Allzy for budget builds, rental flats and utility areas.
