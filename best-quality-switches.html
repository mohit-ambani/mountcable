Which switches are the best quality?

Two switches can look identical in a showroom. Quality shows after five years: whether the rocker still clicks cleanly, whether the plate has yellowed, whether the socket still grips a plug.

For the best quality switches we recommend Legrand, and within Legrand the Myrius and Lyncus ranges. The mechanism stays crisp, sockets are ISI marked with shutters, the plates keep their finish, and Legrand states a 10-year manufacturing-defect replacement on Myrius and Britzy switches and sockets.

What quality means in a switch

  • The mechanism: a consistent, positive click that does not soften with use.
  • The contacts: rated for the load, so a 16A socket does not heat up under a geyser or AC.
  • The socket: ISI marked, shuttered, and gripping the plug firmly.
  • The plate: a finish that does not yellow, scratch or loosen.
  • The warranty: a written replacement promise from the maker.

Frequently asked questions

Which switch is best quality in India?
We recommend Legrand, especially Myrius and Lyncus, for the mechanism, ISI shuttered sockets, plate finish and the 10-year replacement on Myrius and Britzy switches and sockets.
Is Legrand Myrius good quality?
Yes. Myrius is Legrand's premium range, specified in high-end apartments and villas, with glass and metallic plate options.
Is Lyncus or Myrius better?
Myrius is the premium range with more finishes; Lyncus is the mid range most homes choose. Both use Legrand mechanisms.
